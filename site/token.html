02 / THE TOKEN

What it is, what it is not.

What $WINK is

  • The community token of the Hoodwink project, on Robinhood Chain.
  • A way to back the work: RPC capacity, new launchpads fingerprinted, new checks.
  • Subject to the same scan as any other token. Scan it before you buy it.

What $WINK is not

  • Not a key: every scan works without holding it.
  • Not used inside the scanner, the probe contract or the checks.
  • Not a share, a revenue right or a promise of price.

Spotting a fake $WINK

  • The only address is the one shown on this page and pinned on our X account.
  • Anyone can launch a token called WINK. Compare the full address, not the name.
  • We never DM first, never run "migrations", never ask for approvals.
